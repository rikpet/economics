Renaming a category also updates the transactions and issuer rules that use it. Hidden categories are left out of the budget, the charts and the Home Assistant sensors, but keep their transactions. Removing a category leaves its transactions under “Uncategorized”.

Type decides the totals under the budget: Expense and Fixed expense count as expenses, and fixed ones are left out of “Expenses excl. fixed costs”; Transfer / savings is shown as a cost but not counted as an expense; Income counts positive amounts as income. 0 monthly flags every finished month where the category does not add up to 0, e.g. outlays that should be paid back.
